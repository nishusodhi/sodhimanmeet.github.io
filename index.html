PhD Project

Microgel-stabilized aqueous foams

My doctoral research examines how PNIPAM microgel composition, charge and crosslinking control particle mechanics, interfacial organization and foam structure.

  • AFM force spectroscopy and nanomechanical mapping of soft polymer particles.
  • DLS, zeta potential and microscopy for particle and dispersion characterization.
  • SANS/SAXS and neutron reflectometry for foam films and confined structures.
  • Structure-property relationships across particle, interface and foam length scales.

Master Thesis

Graphene and hBN growth on copper

At ESRF and CEA, I investigated graphene and hexagonal boron nitride growth on liquid and single-crystal copper using operando imaging and synchrotron X-ray techniques.

  • Synchrotron XRD and XRR for interfacial structure and thin-film growth.
  • Python-based video analysis of graphene-flake motion and self-organization.
  • Collaboration with beamline scientists on measurement strategy and interpretation.
