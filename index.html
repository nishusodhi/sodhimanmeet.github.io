About

I am a PhD researcher at the Technical University of Darmstadt and the Institut Laue-Langevin in Grenoble. My work focuses on soft polymer colloids in aqueous systems and at fluid interfaces, using particle characterization, AFM and neutron/X-ray scattering to understand how particle composition and mechanics govern interfacial organization, confined structures and macroscopic material response.

Current research

Soft colloidal particles at fluid interfaces

My PhD research investigates how microgel composition, charge and deformability govern their organization at air–water interfaces, and how this interfacial structure translates into thin-film architecture and macroscopic stability.
