About

I am a PhD researcher at the Technical University of Darmstadt and the Institut Laue-Langevin in Grenoble. My work focuses on polymer colloids, soft interfaces and advanced characterization methods, with a particular interest in how particle-scale properties shape foam structure and material performance.

Current work

Microgel-stabilized aqueous foams

I am currently preparing a manuscript on the multiscale structure of microgel-containing foams, linking particle mechanics, interfacial organization and confined foam films.
